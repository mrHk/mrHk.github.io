Who I Am

I have joined Motion Twin in 2007 and spent more than 10 years as a partner developing independent games. Working as a concept artist, animator, VFX artist, game designer, and programmer.

Over the years has released more than 40 games, beeing published on a dozen of books and worked ona few music video aand even a movie.

I work now as freelance a pixel artist and works with teams around the world, specializing in neo-retro independent games. He recently worked on 198X, Steel Assault, MainFrames, and The Escapist 2.
